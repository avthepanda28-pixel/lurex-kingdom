About Lurex Kingdom

More than just another gaming server.

Welcome to Lurex Kingdom! If you're looking for people to play games with, want to discover fun and recommended games, or simply want to find an active community to spend time in, you'll find it here.


Lurex Kingdom is built around gaming, entertainment, community and friendship. Members can meet new people, find players to game with, discuss their favorite games, discover new experiences and participate in a variety of community activities.


The Kingdom also hosts weekly events and daily giveaways, giving members plenty of opportunities to participate and get involved. Whether you're looking for a new game, searching for teammates, or simply want somewhere to hang out, there is always something to discover.


Trading is also welcomed within the community, allowing members to trade supported game items with one another. However, cross-trading and black-market activity are not supported within Lurex Kingdom.


Our goal is to continue growing Lurex Kingdom into an active, entertaining and welcoming gaming community where members can meet others, enjoy games together and create memorable experiences.

Community Standards

Help make Lurex Kingdom a fun and welcoming place.

  • Respect other members of the community.
  • Follow the rules established within the Discord server.
  • Cross-trading is not supported by Lurex Kingdom.
  • Black-market activity is not supported.
  • Do not scam, exploit or intentionally harm other members.
  • Keep conversations appropriate and respectful.
  • Do not intentionally cause unnecessary problems within the community.
  • Have fun and help make the Kingdom better.
