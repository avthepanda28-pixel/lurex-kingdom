About Lurex Kingdom

More than just another gaming community.

Welcome to Lurex Kingdom! If you're looking for people to play games with or want to discover fun and recommended games, you've come to the right place.


Lurex Kingdom is a community created around gaming, entertainment, friendship, and having a place where everyone can find something enjoyable to participate in. Whether you're an experienced player or simply looking for something new, there is always something happening within the Kingdom.


Members can join gaming sessions, meet other players, discover new experiences, participate in weekly events, and take part in exciting daily giveaways.


Trading is also welcomed within the community. However, cross-trading and black-market activity are not supported by Lurex Kingdom.


Our goal is to continue building Lurex Kingdom into an active, entertaining, friendly, and welcoming gaming community.

Community Standards

Help keep Lurex Kingdom fun, welcoming, and safe.

  • Respect other members of the community.
  • Follow the rules established within the Discord server.
  • Cross-trading is not supported by Lurex Kingdom.
  • Black-market activity is not supported.
  • Do not scam, exploit, or intentionally harm other members.
  • Keep conversations appropriate and respectful.
  • Do not intentionally cause unnecessary problems.
  • Have fun and help make the community better.
