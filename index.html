About Lurex Kingdom

More than just another gaming community.

Welcome to Lurex Kingdom! If you're looking for people to play games with, meet new friends, or discover fun and recommended games, you've come to the right place. Lurex Kingdom is a community created around gaming, entertainment, friendship, and having a place where everyone can find something enjoyable to participate in.


Members can join gaming sessions, talk with other players, discover new experiences, participate in weekly events, and take part in exciting daily giveaways. Whether you're an experienced player or simply looking for something new to play, there is always something happening within the Kingdom.


Trading is also welcomed within the community, allowing members to trade items and interact with other players. However, cross-trading and black-market activity are not supported by Lurex Kingdom.

Community Standards

Help us keep Lurex Kingdom fun, welcoming, and safe.

  • Respect other members of the community.
  • Follow the rules established within the Discord server.
  • Cross-trading is not supported by Lurex Kingdom.
  • Black-market activity is not supported.
  • Do not scam, exploit, or intentionally harm other members.
  • Keep conversations appropriate and respectful.
  • Have fun and help make the community better.
