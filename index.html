About Lurex Kingdom

More than just another gaming community.

Welcome to Lurex Kingdom! If you're looking for people to play games with or want to discover fun and recommended games, you'll find it here.


Lurex Kingdom is a community built around gaming, entertainment, friendship and having a place where everyone can find something enjoyable to participate in. Meet new people, find teammates, discover games and become part of an active community.


Members can participate in weekly events and daily giveaways, giving everyone opportunities to get involved and have fun.


Trading is also welcomed within the community. However, cross-trading and black-market activity are not supported by Lurex Kingdom.


Our goal is to continue growing Lurex Kingdom into an active, entertaining and welcoming gaming community.

Community Standards

Help keep Lurex Kingdom fun, welcoming and safe.

  • Respect other members of the community.
  • Follow the rules established within the Discord server.
  • Cross-trading is not supported by Lurex Kingdom.
  • Black-market activity is not supported.
  • Do not scam, exploit or intentionally harm other members.
  • Keep conversations appropriate and respectful.
  • Do not intentionally cause unnecessary problems.
  • Have fun and help make the community better.
